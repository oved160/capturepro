CapturePro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: CapturePro does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your device, inside your browser.

What CapturePro does

CapturePro is a Chrome extension that takes screenshots of web pages, records your screen, microphone and camera when you ask it to, and lets you annotate and save the results.

Data we collect

None. CapturePro has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It makes no network requests of its own and contains no remote code.

How your captures are handled

  • Screenshots, recordings and edits are processed entirely in your browser.
  • Captures are held in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) so that you can view, edit and download them. The eight most recent are kept; older ones are removed automatically.
  • You can delete any capture at any time from the viewer, or remove all of them by removing the extension.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you download or copy a file yourself and share it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Active tab and scripting: to capture and scroll the page you are on, only when you click the extension or use its shortcut.
  • Unlimited storage: recordings can be large and are kept locally until you download or delete them.
  • Access to all websites (optional): requested only if you turn on "Highlight cursor & clicks", so that highlight can be drawn on the pages you visit while recording. You can decline it, and you can revoke it at any time in the extension's settings.
  • Microphone, camera and screen: accessed only after you grant permission in your browser, and only while you are recording or previewing.

Third parties

CapturePro does not use any third-party services, and no data is shared with third parties.
